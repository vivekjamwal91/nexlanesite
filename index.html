The Problem

Content matters. Consistency doesn't happen.

01

Every brand knows blogging builds organic traffic

Product-linked content drives qualified visitors who are already searching for what you sell. It compounds over time in a way that paid ads don't.

02

But publishing consistently is genuinely hard

Between inventory, operations, and customer support — content always gets pushed to next week. Next week never comes.

03

Generic AI tools don't know your products

Existing AI writers produce content that could've been written for any store in your category. It lacks the product specificity that earns rankings and reader trust.

04

AI search is changing how buyers discover products

ChatGPT, Perplexity, and AI-driven search are pulling answers from structured, product-aware content. Stores without it are becoming invisible.

Why This Matters Now

Search isn't a list of links anymore

Buyers are asking questions, not typing keywords. When someone asks an AI assistant "what are the best running shoes for flat feet under $150," the answer comes from structured, product-specific content — not category pages.

Generic content that lacks product depth doesn't get cited. Stores with detailed, well-organised product articles do. This is what AI-assisted discovery rewards.

The brands that start building this content library now will have a structural advantage that's hard to replicate quickly. ToBeNamed is designed specifically for that.
